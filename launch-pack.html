Irish Academic Opportunities Finder — quick school rollout

Use this resource with TY groups, senior-cycle students, subject departments, clubs, guidance programmes and academic noticeboards.

Suggested 10-minute launch

1

Scan/open

Ask students to open the finder from the QR code or school link.

2

Choose school year

Apply the school-year filter first.

3

Search one genuine interest

Examples: medicine, engineering, writing, maths, art, finance, sport, coding.

4

Save three

Each student saves three opportunities worth revisiting.

5

Check the official source

Open one record and show students where the official link, dates and verification information appear.
