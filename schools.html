For schools, TY coordinators & guidance teams

Give students one place to find serious academic opportunities.

The finder brings together competitions, TY and enrichment programmes, summer opportunities, scholarships and funding relevant to secondary-school students in Ireland. It is designed to be shared directly with students without requiring an account.

A three-minute student start

1. Choose your route

Start with competitions, programmes, funding, or search everything together. Students do not need to know the exact opportunity name.

2. Add the filters that matter

School year, subject, geography, cost, delivery mode, entry route and deadline filters turn a very large database into a useful shortlist.

3. Save, verify and apply

Save interesting records, inspect the source and verification details, then confirm the final requirements on the official provider page before applying.

Launch it in a class, year group or guidance programme

1

Share the link or QR code

Post it in Google Classroom, Teams, your school website, a guidance newsletter or a physical noticeboard.

2

Ask students to start with school year + one interest

For example: TY + medicine, 5th Year + maths, 6th Year + scholarship, or free + coding. This produces much better results than browsing randomly.

3

Use Teacher mode for group shortlists

Teacher mode contains fast presets for TY, free, international and closing-soon opportunities. Filter a set, then use Print shortlist or Copy shortlist.

4

Use Upcoming for deadline planning

The calendar combines application deadlines, programme starts and competition dates. Exact unpublished dates are not invented.

What schools can rely on

Official-source research

Records are researched against provider or organiser sources, with source provenance and verification information exposed in the opportunity detail view.

No student account required

The public finder can be used without creating an account. Saved opportunities are kept in the student's own browser storage.

Dates labelled by confidence

Confirmed dates are distinguished from expected, provisional or merely recorded dates. TBA dates stay out of the exact-date calendar.

Corrections can be reported

Each opportunity has an anonymous problem-report option for broken links, changed dates, eligibility corrections, discontinued records or missing information.
